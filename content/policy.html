Your projects

Miniature Studio stores imported model copies, paintings, layers, thumbnails, paint recipes, notes and settings locally on your device. You do not need a Miniature Studio account. Optional iCloud Projects can sync saved projects through your own Apple Account; it is off by default. Projects are not uploaded to an EllisGaming-operated service.

Device backups may include app data according to your device settings. Export backups of important projects before removing the app.

Optional iCloud Projects

If you enable “Sync projects with iCloud Drive”, the app stores copies of your saved models, paintings, layers, recipes and notes in your Apple Account’s iCloud storage. This uses Apple’s iCloud Drive service, your internet connection and your available iCloud storage. Your other devices must use the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive enabled. EllisGaming does not receive or have access to these private iCloud projects through the syncing feature.

Sync pauses while a model is open and resumes after you close it and return to the library. A transfer already handed to iCloud may finish in the background. Downloaded projects remain usable offline, and saved changes are queued until syncing is available. Undo history and device preferences stay local.

Turning sync off keeps your local projects and does not itself delete copies already stored in iCloud. Older saved revisions may remain in iCloud as project history. If different devices edit the same project offline, the latest saved version becomes the main painting when they reconnect; their brushstrokes are not merged. See the iCloud help guide and the deletion guidance below.

Apple handles iCloud data under its own terms and privacy policy. You can manage iCloud access and storage in your device’s Apple Account settings.

Collection and tracking

The app does not include advertising, tracking or a developer-operated analytics service. It does not ask for your name, email address or location to paint. Apple may process purchases and diagnostics under its own policies and your settings.

Importing and sharing

You choose which files to import and where to export or share your work. Cloud storage providers and other destination apps handle information according to their own policies. Download source models before travelling if you want to paint offline.

Information used locally

The app stores preferences, measures time intervals for painting and reads modification dates of its own project files to refresh previews. These uses support the app and are not used to track you.

Deleting your work

With iCloud Projects enabled, deleting a project from the library also queues its removal from iCloud and your other synced devices when they next connect. If that project is open on another device, it is removed after closing it. The app removes its cloud snapshots after the deletion record uploads. A small record containing the project identifier remains in iCloud to prevent an older device from restoring the deleted project; it does not contain the model or painting.

With sync off, deletion stays local and does not delete existing iCloud copies. Turning sync off or removing the app is not a way to erase cloud projects. Exported, shared and backed-up copies are separate and must be managed at their destinations. Removing the app can remove its local data. Export important work before deleting it.
