Start your first project

Choose Paint Demo Marine to start with the included model. To use your own STL, choose Prepare STL in the library and select the file. Review its dimensions and preparation settings, prepare it, then choose Paint This Miniature. Keep your original source file.

Paint, rotate and zoom

With touch, choose Paint mode for brushstrokes and Move mode for navigation. On iPad with Apple Pencil, the Pencil paints, one finger rotates, two fingers move and pinching zooms. Apple Pencil is optional; touch painting works on both iPhone and iPad.

Set up your workspace

Open the palette button to choose paint and adjust the brush. Drag the panel by its handle to move it, or close it for more canvas space. Open it again from the palette button.

Choose a brand and mix a colour

In the Paint Library, use the brand selector to choose Citadel, The Army Painter or Vallejo, then select a range. You can search for a paint name or product code and save favourites.

Choose Mix colours, add two to four supported paints, then set the parts for each ingredient. For example, three parts blue and one part white is a 3:1 mix. Ingredients can come from different brands. Choose how to apply the mix, give it a name and select Save & Use. Find it again in Mixes; its recipe retains the paints and proportions.

Clear mediums and reference-only effects cannot be mixed. Digital colours are estimates, not exact predictions of how physical paints blend.

Build up your painting

Begin with a basecoat, add layers and explore shades and contrast-style paints. The drybrush coverage control moves from light edge-catching strokes to broader raised-surface coverage. Selecting a spray paint lets you cover the whole model with one tap, so use it when you intend to coat every surface.

Take it offline

Download source models and any iCloud projects you want to use before travelling, and check that they open on that device. Core preparation, painting and local saving work without internet. iCloud syncing, online file providers and online sharing need a connection. Offline edits stay local until you reconnect and close the model.

Save and back up

Use the Save button on the painting screen whenever you want a save point. In version 1.7, automatic saving runs every five minutes and when leaving a project or moving the app into the background. Return to the library and allow saving to finish before closing the app. Force-quitting or a crash can lose changes that have not finished saving.

“Undo saved step” returns to the previous save point, so strokes between saves share that step. Tap Save before a change you want to undo separately.

Export an editable Miniature Studio project to keep an independent backup. iCloud sync is not a backup: deletions and newer edits can carry across devices. Exported models and images are separate from editable projects. Before deleting the app or replacing a device, back up any work you want to keep.

Use iCloud Projects on iPhone and iPad

Optional iCloud Projects is available in version 1.7. On each device, sign in to the same Apple Account, enable iCloud Drive and allow Miniature Studio to use it in Settings. Then open iCloud Projects from the app’s library and enable “Sync projects with iCloud Drive”. No separate Miniature Studio account is needed.

  1. Paint and save on one device, then close the model and return to the library.
  2. Keep the app open with an internet connection and check the iCloud status. Large models can take longer to transfer.
  3. Open the library on the other device and allow the project to download before opening it.

Syncing pauses while a model is open, including incoming updates, and resumes in the library. The app checks automatically; Sync now is available if you want to request another check. Models, paintings, layers, recipes and notes sync; undo history and device preferences stay local.

Finish syncing before switching devices where possible. If you make different edits to the same project on two devices while offline, the latest saved version becomes the main painting when they reconnect. Strokes from the two versions are not combined. Other revisions remain in iCloud’s stored history, but you should keep your own exported backups.

Delete a synced project

With iCloud Projects enabled, deleting a model removes it from your other synced devices when they next connect. A model open on another device is removed after closing it. Offline deletions wait for a connection. Export a backup first if you might want the work again.

With sync off, deletion affects only that device. Turning sync off keeps local projects and does not remove copies already in iCloud. Uninstalling the app does not delete your iCloud projects.

If iCloud is not updating

  • Check that both devices use version 1.7 or later, the same Apple Account, and have iCloud Drive and the app’s iCloud Projects switch enabled.
  • Close the model on both devices and leave the libraries open while the transfer completes.
  • Check your connection, available iCloud storage and free storage on both devices. Read the iCloud status and try Sync now.
  • After changing Apple Accounts, review the account and enable syncing again if you want to use it.

If the problem continues, save locally and export a backup before trying further changes. Send support the iCloud status message and your app and system versions. Do not send your Apple Account password or verification codes.

Export a model or a backup

On the home screen, open a model’s three-dot menu and choose Export. You can also export from the painting screen’s three-dot menu.

  • Coloured OBJ + textures (ZIP): unzip the download, then import Miniature.obj. Keep Miniature.mtl and the PNG textures in the same folder: they carry the materials and painting. Enable material and texture loading in your destination app.
  • Painted model (USDZ): share the painted model with a compatible viewer.
  • Print geometry (STL, no colours): export the shape for printing. STL does not store paint colours.
  • Editable Miniature Studio project: keep a backup that you can reopen for painting.

For OBJ and STL, choose the overall height in millimetres, including the base. Exports use the saved working model; they cannot restore detail removed during preparation. Colour printing requires compatible software and hardware. Check dimensions, wall thickness, holes and supports in your slicer.

If a model won’t open

Check that it is a supported file and fully downloaded. Very dense models can require a lighter working mesh; fine geometric details may change when reduced. Texture resolution and model complexity are limited by your device’s memory. Keep your original file and send support the exact error message.

If saving fails

Keep your project open, free some device storage and try saving again. Avoid force-closing or deleting the app while a save is pending.
