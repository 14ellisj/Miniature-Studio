Exporting and printing

Exports contain the saved working model. They cannot restore detail removed during preparation. Keep your original source model and an editable project backup.

  • Painted OBJ: keep the OBJ, MTL and texture images together after extracting the ZIP. The OBJ alone does not contain the painted appearance.
  • Painted USDZ: appearance depends on the receiving app’s material and texture support.
  • STL: geometry only, without paint colours.

Check dimensions, wall thickness, holes, supports and mesh suitability in your slicer. Colour printing requires compatible software and hardware; a coloured export does not make a standard single-material printer reproduce the painting. Follow your printer and material suppliers’ safety instructions.

Saving and backups

Core preparation and painting work locally. Download cloud-hosted source files before travelling. Back up important editable projects outside the app before removing it or changing device. Exported copies and device backups are managed separately.
